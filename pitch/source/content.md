# MetroQuest pitch content

1. **MetroQuest / Strava for Metro** — Pune, one station at a time.
2. **The commuter problem** — A repeated route can lack visible personal
   progress. This is a product hypothesis to test, not claimed interview evidence.
3. **The MetroQuest loop** — Log a ride; collect boarding/exit stops; earn XP;
   find your line through local posts and reactions. Show the actual updated
   Passport screenshot alongside the native editable product loop.
4. **A working product, today** — One Expo app; working web and Android demo;
   24 tests passing as supplied by the engineering lead at pitch creation; iOS
   bundle exports, iOS runtime untested. Show actual updated one-ride Home.
5. **Start with a Pune pilot** — Proposed campus/office audience on Purple/Aqua
   routes. Test repeat logging and community participation. Employer commute
   challenges and optional premium passport are ideas to validate, not revenue.
6. **Try one ride / Help shape the pilot** — Live demo and invitation to Pune
   campus/office pilot partners.
