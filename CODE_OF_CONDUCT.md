# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want MetroQuest to stay a respectful place for anyone contributing to this Pune Metro hackathon demo — in issues, PRs, community posts in the app, and team chat.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Label sample/demo data clearly; do not invent live ridership or partnerships.
- Do not share private personal data of riders or teammates.

## Unacceptable

Harassment, hate, spam, or posting others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers (repo owner / team lead). Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation.

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
